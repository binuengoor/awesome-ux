Google Gemini iOS Ambient Light Spec

Living, Breathing Light.

Modeled directly after Google Gemini on iOS. An organic ambient light that gently breathes, ebbs, flows, and recedes into pure OLED black.

Live Shader Controls WebGL ACTIVE • 60 FPS
Breathing Speed 1.0x
Peak Luminescence 0.95

True OLED Black Fidelity

Rest envelopes spend 30–35% of each cycle completely at 0.0, allowing pixels on OLED displays to completely turn off.

Ultra-Soft Gaussian Math

Zero rainbow banding or harsh edges. Light is synthesized via exponential falloff with chromatic simplex coordinate warping.

1 Draw Call & Zero CPU Overhead

DPR is clamped to 1.25x for battery efficiency. The render loop automatically suspends when the tab is hidden or backgrounded.
